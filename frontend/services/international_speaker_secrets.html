📖 Course Overview

5 Sessions. 1 Hour Each. Live Online / In-Person / Hybrid.

What You'll Walk Away With: 50+ scripted phrases, 5 proven opening techniques, 10+ story structures, and the confidence to step onto any stage—anywhere in the world.

🎯 Session 1: The Volunteer Mindset ▼

"Be the first to raise your hand—anywhere, anytime."

Learn the 25+ phrases to volunteer, share your perspective, ask questions, and step onto the stage—even when you're nervous.

Key Takeaways:

  • ✅ "I'll go first."
  • ✅ "I'm nervous, but I'm going anyway."
  • ✅ "If I may, I'd like to share a different perspective."
  • ✅ Cultural adaptations for direct & indirect cultures
🔥 Session 2: The 30-Second Hook ▼

"Your first 30 seconds determine everything—across cultures."

Master 5 proven openers that work every time: Question, Story, Statistic, Provocative, Personal.

Key Takeaways:

  • ✅ "Raise your hand if you've ever felt completely unprepared..."
  • ✅ "I was in Japan, 2003, in a room with delegates from 64 countries..."
  • ✅ "Everything you've been told about public speaking is wrong..."
📖 Session 3: The Instant Story ▼

"Create a story on the spot that connects with any audience."

Use the 3-part story formula: Character, Challenge, Resolution. Learn to tell stories that are remembered.

Key Takeaways:

  • ✅ "Let me tell you a story about a time I completely failed..."
  • ✅ "I used to be terrified of speaking in front of people..."
  • ✅ "And that's why I always say..."
🎤 Session 4: Multi-Sensory Delivery ▼

"Use voice, body, eyes, and gestures to command the room."

Master the 4 tools of the international speaker: Voice, Body, Eyes, and Gestures.

Key Takeaways:

  • ✅ Voice: Tone, pitch, pace, volume
  • ✅ Body: Posture, movement, presence
  • ✅ Eyes: Eye contact, connection
  • ✅ Gestures: Hands, emphasis
🏆 Session 5: The Finale Effect ▼

"Deliver a closing that gets people on their feet—in any language."

Use the Finale Formula: Pause, Final Sentence, Look, Stand. Master 15 complete finales you can use immediately.

Key Takeaways:

  • ✅ "So here's my challenge to you..."
  • ✅ "The only way to become a great speaker is to speak."
  • ✅ "I've spoken in 35 countries..."
  • ✅ "The stage is waiting. You are ready. Now go."
